Actual Hrs auto-fill from the Payworks timesheet for each matched driver on that date (shown shaded green — type to override); Proj Hrs and notes are entered manually and saved in this browser per day. Payworks posts a day or two behind, so the most recent dates may be blank until entered. Productivity colour: green ≥ $200/hr, yellow $175–$199, red < $175. Target rate is adjustable above.
Truck Fill estimates how full each truck is on its delivery (D) and pickup (P) legs — category quantities × rough cube factors, vs a per-business-unit “full” line (90th percentile of historical truck-day delivery loads). Green ≤85%, amber 85–100%, red >100% (overloaded). Hover an icon for cube, fill% and the top categories on that leg. A big D high / P low gap = out full, back empty.
